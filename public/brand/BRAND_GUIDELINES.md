\# 林間小鎮 ForestWork — Brand Assets Guidelines



\## 1. SVG Asset Summary (矢量资源清单)

\- \*\*Emblem (图案标)\*\*: `logo-light.svg` (Dark stroke) / `logo-dark.svg` (White stroke)

\- \*\*EN Logotype (英文字标)\*\*: `text-en-light.svg` / `text-en-dark.svg` (Tight ViewBox: `0 0 266 36`)

\- \*\*ZH Logotype (中文字标)\*\*: `text-zh-light.svg` / `text-zh-dark.svg` (Tight ViewBox: `0 0 400 90`)



\## 2. Display Minimums (最小渲染高度)

\- \*\*Header Icon + Text\*\*: `h-3.5` \~ `h-4` (Text) | `h-7` (Emblem)

\- \*\*Horizontal Lockup (横向组合)\*\*: Min height `45px`

\- \*\*Vertical Lockup (直向双语组合)\*\*: Min height `75px` (Emblem `h-14` > ZH `h-5` > EN `h-3.5`)



\## 3. Usage Rules (使用铁律)

\- Do not stretch, distort, or modify SVG ViewBox aspect ratios.

\- Always use `light` variants on white/light backgrounds (#FFFFFF / #F9FAFB).

\- Always use `dark` variants on dark/night backgrounds (#090A0F / #12141A).
